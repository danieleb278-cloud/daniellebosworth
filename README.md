# Danielle Bosworth | Portfolio

The source code behind [madebydanielleb.com](https://madebydanielleb.com), my portfolio for product, customer experience, AI, and systems-focused work.

I built this portfolio to do more than collect finished projects. It shows how I approach problems: understanding what is actually happening, finding connections across people and systems, testing ideas, and working through the constraints that appear once an idea becomes real.

## What's Inside

The portfolio brings together work across product design, research, customer experience, operations, marketing, and AI-assisted product development.

Featured work includes:

- **Vocari** - An evolving career discovery product that became an exploration of how a system can learn from changing and sometimes contradictory human evidence.
- **Joomla Information Architecture** - Research and redesign work focused on helping users find extensions based on intent rather than requiring them to already know the right terminology.
- **Commercial Work** - Examples connecting product, customer, marketing, and operational decisions to measurable business outcomes.
- **Additional Projects** - Product concepts, UX research, strategy work, and experiments that show different parts of my problem-solving process.

## How I Built It

The portfolio itself has become an ongoing product project.

I began with rapid AI-assisted prototyping to move from ideas to working interactions quickly. As the site and individual case studies became more complex, my workflow expanded beyond the original visual builder and into the underlying codebase.

Today that workflow includes:

- Lovable for rapid prototyping and interface iteration
- GitHub for version control and direct codebase work
- AI-assisted development for debugging, implementation, and working through technical constraints
- Supabase for backend functionality where needed
- Vercel and automated deployment workflows
- Responsive testing and iterative refinement across the live experience

The goal was never to become a software engineer. I wanted enough understanding of the technology underneath my ideas to make better product decisions, communicate across disciplines, and keep solving problems when the first tool or approach was no longer enough.

## Technology

Built with React, TypeScript, TanStack Start, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, and supporting UI libraries.

## Why the Repository Is Public

The finished site shows the work. The repository provides another layer of evidence for how I experiment, iterate, manage changes, and work through the technical side of bringing product ideas to life.
